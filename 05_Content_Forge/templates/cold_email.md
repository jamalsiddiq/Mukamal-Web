# Cold Email Template: The Surgical Audit
**Goal:** High-converting cold outreach to Heads of Product/Design. No fluff, straight to the value.

**Subject Line Options:**
- Your [Specific Flow] is dropping [Estimated %] of users
- QA: UX issue on [Brand Name]'s [Specific Page]
- Mukamal Audit: [Brand Name] vs [Competitor Name]

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

I'll keep this under 30 seconds.

We ran a UX heuristic audit on [Brand Name]'s [Specific Flow] this week. 

**The data:**
- Issue 1: [Specific heuristic failure, e.g., "Contrast ratio on your primary CTA fails WCAG AAA."]
- Issue 2: [Specific friction point, e.g., "Your onboarding flow has 3 unnecessary cognitive loads compared to [Competitor]."]

We are Mukamal. We build high-converting, brutalist digital products. We've mapped out the exact fix for this in a Figma prototype.

Are you open to seeing it? No pitch, just the data.

Best,
[Your Name]
Mukamal
[Website Link]
