# Content Calendar: The Mukamal Output
**Goal:** Maintain a consistent, high-value content cadence across channels.

## Weekly Cadence (The 3x Framework)

### Tuesday: The Data Drop (LinkedIn & Twitter)
- **Format:** Single stark statistic or competitive matrix screenshot.
- **Topic:** A shocking heuristic failure from a recent audit or industry benchmark.
- **Objective:** Engagement and brand authority.

### Thursday: The Surgical Teardown (LinkedIn Carousel / Twitter Thread)
- **Format:** Step-by-step breakdown of a specific flow (Problem -> Diagnosis -> Fix).
- **Topic:** Deep dive into a specific case study (e.g., MetaMask Seed Phrase).
- **Objective:** Showcase the "Designing Systems" capability.

### Friday: The Weekly Audit (Newsletter)
- **Format:** Email delivering one clear, actionable UX insight.
- **Topic:** Repurposed from Thursday's teardown or a quick new insight.
- **Objective:** Lead nurturing and conversion.
