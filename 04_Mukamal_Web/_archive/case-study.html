Clinical Diagnoses

THE REVENUE
LEAKS

80% of audited brands suffer from preventable anti-patterns that directly increase bounce rates and kill conversion.

LEAK 01: CONTENT-BLOCKING WALLS

Cookie banners and CAPTCHA barriers cover 15-20% of the viewport, creating active friction before the user even sees the product. (Coinbase, Trust Wallet)

LEAK 02: MISSING CONTEXT

Text-only heroes with zero explanation of the product on mobile. Users don't know what they are downloading. (MetaMask)

LEAK 03: UNSOLICITED OVERLAYS

Chat widgets with notification badges and autoplay videos steal attention without consent. (Rainbow, Coinbase)
